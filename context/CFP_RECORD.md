## 4. The full call text (verbatim, from the live Call-for-Papers page)

- **Scope:** "Artificial intelligence (AI), automation, and the Internet of Things are among the most significant drivers of change worldwide." The issue welcomes "accessible, decision-relevant research geared toward informing solutions and strategies relevant to overcoming the challenges linked with next-generation computing and digitalization, particularly implications for energy, climate change, and more broadly resources and the environment."
- **Research spread:** "research spanning diverse fields – from engineering to computer science, to the social sciences and humanities."
- **Article types:** "The Special Issue invites both full-length research articles and shorter perspective and commentary pieces."
- **IPCC AR7 alignment:** "The 2026-27 timeline ensures compatibility with the literature cut-off dates of the IPCC 7th Assessment Report process, and relevant papers are expected to form an important knowledge base for several chapters within the AR7."
- **Review model:** "Papers in the Special Issue will be reviewed, revised, and published on a rolling basis."
